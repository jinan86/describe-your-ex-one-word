## The word

**earworm**

An ex who, like a song you cannot shake, stays lodged in your head
long after they are gone. It is also, literally, a worm — a tiny thing
that burrows in and will not leave. Everyone has had a song stuck in
their head; everyone has had an ex stuck in theirs.

## Why this word

The field is saturated with software metaphors — *unsubscribe*,
*recalled*, *expired*, *uninstalled*, *refund* — so this entry steps
outside the tech frame entirely. *Earworm* names a specific lived
experience, carries a double meaning, and lands as a joke at a glance
without being a plain insult.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt  # earworm
```

`main.py` is stdlib-only and deterministic: it writes exactly one word
plus a newline to `/output/entry.txt`. Verified locally: two runs
produce byte-identical output (`sha256 a4d96fd464e8b4580fb576e91309ce5a667e668163a38f7aeeb49da320d5ce9b`).
